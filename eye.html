Eye Scroll

Scroll this page with your eyes. Everything runs in your browser; the camera image never leaves your device.

  1. Tap Start and allow the front camera.
  2. Tap Calibrate. Look at each red dot until it shrinks. Keep your head fairly still.
  3. Look at the bottom of the screen to scroll down and the top to scroll up. The farther toward the edge, the faster.
Tap Start to begin.
